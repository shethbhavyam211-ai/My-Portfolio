CubeSat Micro-Satellites: The Future of Democratized Space

A CubeSat is a standard miniaturized satellite consisting of 10×10×10 cm units (1U). In modern space missions, 3U and 6U configurations allow high-resolution optical sensors, hyperspectral imagers, and edge-AI computers to operate in Low Earth Orbit at a fraction of traditional satellite costs.

  • 🛰️ ADCS (Attitude Determination & Control): Reaction wheels and magnetorquers to keep the satellite pointing accurately toward Earth.
  • ⚡ Power System (EPS): Triple-junction solar arrays yielding 40W–150W with lithium-iron phosphate battery storage.
  • 🧠 Edge AI Computing: Real-time on-orbit filtering of cloud-covered satellite images to save ground communication bandwidth.
